Myth 1: "All oil sprayers work the same."

Regular sprayers use a pump designed for water. Olive oil is 10× more viscous. It physically cannot spray the same way.

Myth 2: "Aerosol cooking spray is fine."

Aerosol spray contains propellants, additives, and lecithin — not just oil. You're spraying chemicals you didn't ask for onto every meal.

Myth 3: "I don't use that much oil anyway."

Most people pour 3–5× more oil than they need. A fine mist covers the same surface with a fraction of the amount. ~1 gram per spray. Most customers use 60–80% less.

Myth 4: "My air fryer doesn't need a sprayer."

Air fryer crispiness is almost entirely about oil coverage. Uneven oil = uneven crispiness. A fine, even mist is the single biggest upgrade you can make.

Myth 5: "I tried a sprayer. They don't work."
